# Issues, blockers and skipped analyses

- **Empty masks and visibility:** 39 laser_diode positive views have nonzero GT entirely outside the joint crop; two are source-all-zero. The 41 paired sheets are provided, but determining whether the image shows the defect requires human review. No labels/masks were repaired.
- **Calibration resolution:** Task 02's 10 normal objects/category cannot distinguish 1%, 5%, and 10%; those thresholds are one repeated maximum. Task 03 uses a pooled 50-normal-object calibration split for a fixed 50/50 normal-rank mean and OR diagnostic, but the objects come from the native test manifests. These are object-disjoint descriptive transfer results, not external/deployment calibration.
- **Fusion tuning:** no independent validation anomalies exist. A validation-selected weighted mean was skipped. Fixed 50/50 unsupervised rank calibration and calibrated OR are reported; no pixel fusion is attempted.
- **Scope:** only five preselected diagnostic categories have paired numeric maps. The 160-category overlap is a screening table over differing native splits. No 160-category paired or ensemble claim is made. The frozen extension list is recorded but no additional category inference was launched.
- **Region operating points:** `region_overlap_vs_fpr.csv` retains Task 02 paired component IoU/coverage and both/d2-only/ArcAD-only/neither region detection at IoU 0.3 for the 1%/5% per-category pixel-FPR targets. Those pixel thresholds were estimated from 10 normal objects/category and are exploratory. A new region-mask OR recomputation with its own pixel-area false-positive union cost was skipped; implementable score-level OR false-positive cost is reported in `operating_point_metrics.csv` and `failure_overlap_vs_fpr.csv`. Pixel/region confidence intervals and ROC/PR bootstrap bands were also skipped.
- **Inference/GPU:** no new model inference was needed, so no CUDA fallback occurred. The existing Task 02 ArcAD and extra D2 maps were generated on RTX 3090; Task 01 low-three D2 maps were CPU-generated. Hashes and provenance are recorded in `protocol_audit.json`.
